Apps for macOS

Small tools that do one job well.

Native Mac apps, built for my own podcast and audio work and shared for anyone with the same problems. Drop a file in, get the right file out.

Signed & notarized

Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Open it with no Gatekeeper warning.

Update themselves

Each app checks GitHub for a new version and installs it in place.

Free & open source

MIT or GPL. Read the code, build it yourself, file an issue.

macOS 15 or later

Built for Apple silicon. DoublEnder also runs on Intel Macs.

Audio

Prep, level, extract, convert, record.

WaxOn/WaxOff

Podcast audio prep

Two modes in one app. WaxOn turns a quiet, uneven guest recording into a clean prep WAV before you edit. WaxOff normalizes the finished mix to your delivery spec.

  • Signal conditioning for ingest
  • Broadcast loudness normalization for delivery
  • Everything stays on your Mac
Download App page Source

ClipHack

Broadcast clip prep

Gets third-party clips (news, promos, broadcast audio) to sit at the same level as the rest of your show.

  • Dynamic leveling across speakers
  • Two-pass EBU R128 loudness to your target
  • Brick-wall peak ceiling; downloads clips from the web
Download Manual Source

FilmStrip

Film audio extraction

Pulls the soundtrack out of movies and TV episodes so you can listen to them like audiobooks.

  • Scans MKV, MP4, MOV and AVI containers
  • Picks the right language and track for you
  • Writes portable audio files
Download Manual Source

Transcoder

Audio converter

Drop files in, pick a format, convert. From a video it takes the first audio track and nothing else.

  • MP3, AAC, Apple Lossless, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, Opus
  • Presets per format; batch queue with per-file progress
  • Tags and cover art carried over
Download Manual Source

DoublEnder

Guest recorder

Send it to a podcast guest. They pick a microphone, press the one button, and send you the file. No setup, no account, no cloud.

  • Records AAC to the Desktop
  • Runs from anywhere, no installer
  • Apple silicon and Intel

Utilities

Keys, secrets and passwords.

KeyVault

Keys & secrets

One window over your SSH, GPG and Age keys, plus an encrypted store for API keys, notes and files under a master passphrase.

  • Finds, shows and generates keys
  • Secrets kept in the macOS Keychain
  • Backup any gpg can open, with or without KeyVault
Download Manual Source

Perfect Passwords Grabber

Password fetcher

Fetches three fresh, truly random passwords from GRC's Ultra High Security Password Generator and copies one with a click.

  • 64-char hex, 63-char ASCII, 63-char alphanumeric
  • Nothing stored, reused or logged
  • Built-in help on which format to use
